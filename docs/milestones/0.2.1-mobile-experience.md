# Milestone 0.2.1: Mobile-first experience

## Outcome

The single-device decision loop feels deliberate and app-like on a phone while remaining complete with touch, pointer, keyboard, screen reader, or reduced motion.

## Product contract

- Phone is the canonical layout: one focused, photo-led card and thumb-reachable actions.
- Swipe left maps to `avoid` and swipe right maps to `prefer`; visible buttons remain the authoritative accessible controls. `accept` is always explicit.
- Fixture artwork is labeled illustrative and never presented as a real venue photo.
- Tablet may combine deck and context. Desktop may combine map, deck, and room activity after live discovery exists.
- Cards do not claim ratings, review counts, dishes, hours, or popularity unless a permitted source supplies that exact field.

## Work

- responsive image-led candidate review and ballot cards;
- bounded horizontal drag with preference stamps and no gesture-only action;
- mobile safe-area and sticky action treatment;
- generated local fixture media with honest source labels;
- mobile, tablet, desktop, keyboard, and reduced-motion evidence;
- installable PWA metadata and icons before milestone exit.

## Exit gate

- No horizontal overflow at 320, 390, 768, or 1440 CSS pixels.
- Core actions meet a 44 CSS-pixel target and remain reachable at 200% zoom.
- Button, keyboard, screen-reader, and reduced-motion paths preserve every decision capability.
- A moderated phone test confirms that at least 8/10 participants understand `avoid`, `accept`, `prefer`, and the difference between illustrative and sourced facts.

## Implementation status

The initial photo-led responsive deck, fixture labels, gesture enhancement, and button fallback are implemented. PWA install artifacts, interaction tuning, responsive screenshots, and moderated testing remain.
