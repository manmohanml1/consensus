# Milestone 0.6.0: Closed beta and operational readiness

## Outcome

Real groups repeatedly reach and act on trustworthy decisions.

## Work

- explicit commitment step and map/reservation handoff;
- privacy-safe analytics contract and voluntary feedback;
- accessibility audit, abuse defense, load/recovery exercises;
- support, incident, backup, deletion and provider-outage runbooks;
- closed beta with at least 30 completed groups.

## Exit gate

The MVP success and guardrail metrics in `docs/product/MVP.md` are measured, severe constraint violations are investigated, operational recovery is rehearsed, and a documented go/pivot/stop decision is made.
