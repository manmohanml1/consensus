# CQ-107: Moderated decision-loop usability acceptance

- Milestone: 0.2.0 acceptance
- Type: research
- GitHub issue: [#129](https://github.com/manmohanml1/consensus/issues/129)
- Status: planned

## Outcome

Verify with representative phone-first sessions that people understand the constraint-first setup, avoid/accept/prefer choices, result explanation, and commitment handoff without facilitator instruction.

## Acceptance

- define a bounded script, participant profile, consent note, and observation rubric without collecting unnecessary personal data;
- run 10 moderated sessions against one immutable Preview or the fixture-only reference deployment;
- at least 8 of 10 participants complete the decision loop without instruction;
- participants distinguish hard constraints from preferences and understand why the result won;
- record aggregate findings without names, precise location, or individual ballot histories;
- convert every release-critical finding into a linked issue and document the milestone pass or deferral rationale.

This acceptance research does not authorize provider provisioning, Production promotion, a tag, or a GitHub Release.
