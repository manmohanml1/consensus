# MVP contract

## Hypothesis

A constraint-aware, explainable short-list decision will get real food groups to commit faster and with less negotiation than their existing group-chat process.

## MVP scope

- food only;
- groups of 2–8;
- host creates a temporary room and shares a link/code;
- participants choose a temporary display name;
- host locks the roster before voting;
- group constraints cover dietary needs, accessibility, maximum price, distance, and open-at-target-time;
- 8–12 candidates from a trustworthy source or explicit host additions;
- button and swipe input with undo until submission closes;
- explainable outcome, runner-up, and no-safe-result state;
- map/share handoff and explicit “I’m in” commitment;
- automatic room expiry and deletion.

## Explicitly deferred

- accounts, group history, push notifications, AI recommendations;
- movies and activities;
- chat, public discovery, sponsored ranking, reservations, or payments;
- profile-based personalization;
- hard realtime latency promises.

## Validation gate

Do not expand categories until at least 30 real groups have completed the flow and the evidence shows:

- at least 60% of rooms with two joins reach a result;
- median decision time is under five minutes;
- fewer than 2% of reported outcomes violate a declared constraint;
- at least 25% of completed groups return within 30 days or explicitly request a repeat workflow;
- qualitative interviews identify decision confidence—not novelty—as the main value.
