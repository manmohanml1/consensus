# Product definition

## Problem

Small groups often agree that they want to do something but stall on selecting a specific option. Group chats distribute suggestions poorly, hide non-negotiables, reward the loudest participant, and end without commitment.

## Promise

Create a room, agree on constraints, react to a short trustworthy list, understand the recommendation, and take the next action in a few minutes.

## Initial users

- couples and friends choosing a nearby meal;
- coworkers selecting lunch;
- families balancing dietary, mobility, cost, and distance needs;
- visitors who lack shared knowledge of an area.

## Jobs to be done

1. “Help us narrow the available options without another long conversation.”
2. “Do not recommend something that violates a real need.”
3. “Give quieter participants equal input.”
4. “Explain the result enough that we trust it.”
5. “Let us act immediately once we decide.”

## Core loop

```text
intent -> non-negotiables -> candidate quality check -> reactions
       -> explainable result -> group commitment -> map/reservation/order
```

## Differentiation

Swipe input is replaceable. The defensible product layer is a reusable decision protocol combining constraint safety, data confidence, explainable fairness, group memory with consent, and measurable conversion from result to action.

## Non-goals before 1.0

- a social network or public feed;
- restaurant reviews or an advertising marketplace;
- autonomous booking or purchasing;
- anonymous public rooms discoverable by strangers;
- surveillance-style behavioral profiles;
- every decision category at launch.

## Success metrics

Primary: percentage of created rooms that reach an accepted plan within five minutes.

Guardrails:

- join completion rate;
- median time to first vote and final decision;
- no-result and abandoned-room rate;
- “result violated a constraint” rate, which must approach zero;
- result-to-map/reservation/order handoff rate;
- seven- and thirty-day repeat-group rate;
- recommendation override rate and stated reason;
- accessibility journey completion and error rates.

Do not optimize raw swipe count, time in app, invitations sent, or notification volume.
